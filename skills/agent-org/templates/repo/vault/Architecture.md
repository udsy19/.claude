# Architecture — what governs the {{PROJECT}} fleet

[[Vision]] says what the *product* is for. This says what the *machine that builds it* is for:
who may decide what, what a mission is, what makes work start and stop, and — the part that
matters — **what actually enforces each rule, as opposed to what is merely written down.**

Every rule carries one verdict, and a verdict is re-measured, never carried forward:

| verdict | meaning |
|---|---|
| **ENFORCED** | a named mechanism refuses the violation; the mechanism is on the board |
| **DECLARED** | written down in a file, and nothing checks it |
| **NEITHER** | not written down anywhere, and not checked |

Verdicts below are what the agent-org kit installs on day one. When a rule's mechanism changes,
update its verdict here with the commit that changed it.

---

## 1. The chain of authority

```
owner ──────── vision · rulings · the definition of done · accepts missions
  └── supervisor ── owns Plan.md and Roadmap.md · assigns work · decides disputes
        └── subagent ── does ONE plan row · writes code, gates, evidence, a session note (lane worker: a report)
                        proposes; never decrees
```

**R1 — The owner owns `vault/Decisions/` and `.claude/rules/`; the supervisor owns `vault/Plan.md`
and `vault/Roadmap.md`.** **ENFORCED at the boundaries; the hook is advisory.**
`scripts/hooks/agent-contract.mjs` (PreToolUse) refuses a subagent's `Edit`/`Write` to those
paths and delivers [[AGENTS]] in the refusal — guidance for an honest agent, not a boundary: it
cannot gate a Bash write. The boundaries are (1) the sandbox, which decides what a lane process can
write at all (`docs/isolation.md` in the kit), and (2) the promotion coordinator, the only process
that moves integration or main: it runs `scripts/gates/plan-ownership.mjs --lane` with main's gate
code, which re-derives from git which commits touched a protected path and refuses a lane's
landing that touches any, whatever its trailers say. On main, the owner's or supervisor's own
commits carry `Authority: owner|supervisor` or `Proposal: #<n>`. One declaration
(`scripts/lib/protected-paths.mjs`) feeds the hook and the gates; `scripts/gates/protected-paths.mjs`
holds every document that restates it.

verbatim · **—** may not write. "Worker" and "sub-agent" are both `ORG_ROLE` unset (subagent).

**What each may NOT write**, in one line each: the owner — nothing is closed to the owner. The
overseer — an owner verdict or acceptance, and a rule or decision the owner did not give. A lane
supervisor — code, the vault, protected paths, `main` unless its lane may land. A worker — any
protected path, `main`, another worker's branch, another session's note, auto-memory. A sub-agent —
everything its worker may not, plus the vault (it reports to its worker).

**R2 — A subagent proposes; it never decrees.** **ENFORCED.**
`scripts/propose.mjs` appends to `vault/_log/proposals.jsonl` in the main checkout (interactive
sessions; a sandboxed lane worker cannot reach it and puts the proposal in its report instead). A `challenge` is refused unless `--why` contains a number; `--resolve` refuses unless
`ORG_ROLE` is supervisor or owner. `scripts/propose.test.mjs` races resolves against filings.

## 2. The mission layer — what the fleet is *for*

**R3 — Exactly one mission is in force, because the owner accepted it.** **DECLARED + checked.**
A mission carries `state: running | paused | blocked-on-human | done` (the GATING field) and
`accepted-by: owner` / `accepted: <date>`. `scripts/loop-state.mjs --check` reds on two running
missions or a `state:` outside the vocabulary. Acceptance itself is not machine-checkable: it is a
trusted-human event (gate-independence law 9) and no agent may perform it.

**R4 — The mission that briefs the fleet is the one named in settings.** **ENFORCED.**
`.claude/settings.json` sets `ORG_MISSION`; the SessionStart hook prints that mission's
`## NOW` … `## ---END-NOW` block into every session; `scripts/loop-guard.sh` gates on that same
mission's `state:`; `loop-state.mjs --check` reds when the block's markers are missing.

**R5 — A mission's NOW block is current.** **DECLARED.** Nothing checks its age. The supervisor
rewrites it whenever the facts change, and archives the old text in a report.

## 3. The plan layer

**R6 — Work is an ordered queue of numbered rows with a closed status vocabulary.** **ENFORCED.**
`scripts/gates/plan-integrity.mjs`: six cells, contiguous numbering, status ∈ {open,
in-flight:<branch>, blocked-on-owner:<Oid>, done:<sha>}, every Roadmap citation graded on its quoted
words AND its line number, landing ticks at the END of the Roadmap, every acceptance cell names an
existing gate or says `gate to write:`.

**R7 — A row may have subtasks, and a parent is not done while a child is open.** **ENFORCED.**
`scripts/gates/plan-hierarchy.mjs`. `scripts/plan-row.mjs <id>` prints one row in full.

**R8 — An agent does the row it was assigned and does not invent the mandate.** **NEITHER.**
Nothing connects "what an agent is doing" to "what the owner asked for" except the dispatch
prompt ([[SUPERVISOR]] §2) and the reviewer.

## 4. The knowledge layer — anti-fluff and anti-duplicate

**R9 — Every vault note is reachable from Home within two hops.** **ENFORCED.**
`scripts/gates/vault-reachability.mjs` (+ `--selftest`); hubs and [[Map]] regenerated by
`scripts/vault-hubs.mjs`, whose `--check` is a board row.

**R10 — "Has this been done before?" costs one page.** **ENFORCED (freshness).**
[[Index]] is generated by `scripts/gen-subject-index.py`; `--check` is a board row.

**R11 — Search before you write; delete what you supersede.** **DECLARED + tooled.**
`.claude/rules/no-bloat.md`, the `pre-edit-scan` skill and `scripts/where.mjs` (main, HEAD and
unlanded branches, content-filtered). No gate can prove a duplicate was NOT written; the reviewer
checks it.

**R12 — A landing may not grow the tracked tree in silence.** **ENFORCED.**
`scripts/gates/sprawl.mjs`: growth needs an `EVIDENCE-GROWTH:` paragraph that names what was added
and says something the gate could not compute; a new `evidence/<dir>/` needs a README naming a real
reader.

**R13 — The laws and their worked cases stay joined.** **ENFORCED.** `scripts/gates/rules-index.mjs`.

## 5. The verification layer

**R14 — The board is the only trusted signal, and its verdict is an exit code.** **ENFORCED.**
`bash scripts/gates/org-board.sh`: 0 green · 1 red · 2 refused (counted red) · 77 a named skip,
never a pass. The project adds its own product gates to its own board; this one guards the org.

**R15 — A session leaves a trail.** **ENFORCED, bounded.** `scripts/loop-guard.sh` (Stop hook)
blocks ending a turn while today's dispatches (recorded independently by `scripts/usage-hook.sh`)
are newer than today's session note — at most three times running, never when the mission is
not `running`, and never for a lane worker (`AGENT_NAME` set), whose report is its trail.
